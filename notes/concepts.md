## Pick: Vice City FM's 13-year request

The radio concept wins on the full combination, not just novelty: GTA fans recognize the in-world station immediately; “same song since 2013” makes the thirteen-year delay a quick, audible joke; and a ghost DJ can deliver the make.fun pitch as the station's own live interruption. The title study lists many official-looking forms, download screens, sick-day jokes and queues, but no DJ or radio-show framing. A record engraved with “WAITING FOR GTA VI” and a bright studio “ON AIR” sign make the setting survive the judge's 480-pixel view, while the central DJ ghost makes the brand part of the scene.
